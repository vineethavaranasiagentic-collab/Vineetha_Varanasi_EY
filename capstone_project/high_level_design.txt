COMMERCIAL BANKING RELATIONSHIP MANAGER COPILOT
HIGH-LEVEL DESCRIPTION

1. PURPOSE

The Commercial Banking Relationship Manager Copilot is an agentic assistant that helps Relationship Managers (RMs) turn fragmented client information into proactive, explainable relationship actions.

The project uses fictional commercial-banking data only.

2. INFORMATION ANALYZED

The Copilot brings together:

- Client financials
- Account behavior and transactions
- Banking product usage
- Loan and covenant status
- CRM and RM notes
- Market and industry news

3. CORE CAPABILITIES

The Copilot can:

- Consolidate fragmented client information into a simple client profile.
- Monitor financial performance, account activity, product usage, covenants, and industry conditions.
- Identify early warning signals such as declining balances, delayed payments, covenant stress, and industry deterioration.
- Identify cross-sell opportunities such as working-capital facilities, cash management, trade finance, and payroll services.
- Identify retention risks from declining balances, reduced product usage, and negative client interactions.
- Retrieve supporting evidence from client documents, CRM notes, transactions, financials, covenants, and industry information.
- Score opportunities using evidence strength, client need, relationship relevance, and timing.
- Generate explainable next-best-actions.
- Prepare concise client meeting briefs.
- Draft client outreach emails.
- Perform basic compliance checks on generated communications.
- Require explicit RM approval before a communication can be marked ready to send.

4. HIGH-LEVEL WORKFLOW

Client Data
    -> Client Profile
    -> Monitoring
    -> Detect Risks and Opportunities
    -> Retrieve Supporting Evidence
    -> Reason and Score
    -> Generate Next-Best-Action
    -> Prepare Meeting Brief and Communication Draft
    -> Compliance Check
    -> RM Approval
    -> Ready to Send or Stop

5. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP CONTROL

The Copilot only drafts communications. It does not independently send client messages.

A communication can be marked READY_TO_SEND only when:

- The recommendation has supporting evidence.
- The communication passes the compliance check.
- The RM explicitly approves it.

Rejected, unsupported, or non-compliant outputs must stop and require review or correction.

6. ROLE OF THE LLM

The LLM may be used for:

- Explaining detected signals
- Summarizing client information
- Reasoning about opportunities
- Generating next-best-actions
- Preparing meeting briefs
- Drafting communications

Calculations, thresholds, basic risk and opportunity detection, compliance controls, approval status, and sending permissions remain outside the LLM in deterministic Python logic.

7. EXPECTED VALUE

The Copilot is intended to reduce RM meeting-preparation effort, improve early risk awareness, surface relevant relationship opportunities, provide evidence-based explanations, and support consistent client outreach.

8. SCOPE BOUNDARY

This is not a full banking platform or an autonomous communication system. It is a focused Commercial Banking Relationship Manager Copilot for fictional data that supports analysis, recommendations, meeting preparation, communication drafting, compliance checking, and human approval.
